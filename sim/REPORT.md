# Heisty Spideys — Monte Carlo Rules Report

Generated by `node sim/run.mjs --runs 5000 --seed 1` (parameter sweeps and fix experiments: 2000 runs per heist per variant). Rulebook v4.5, rulings from book/REVIEW.md §A–B. Every heist is played by a crew of five legal random spiders (Ch 7), start to finish: Planning, the heist obstacles, a generated Escape, the Debrief. Same seed → same report.

**How to read it.** The book leaves many calls to the Storyteller. Each one is a named parameter (§7). Three readings run at full size:

- **default**: the simulator's best reading of the book. Partial = −1 die on the spider's next roll, no Alert: variant **(b)**.
- **generous**: every ambiguous rule read in the crew's favour.
- **strict**: every ambiguous rule read against the crew. That includes Partial = +1 Alert (variant **(a)**), opposed creature rolls, creatures adding their Alert everywhere, two rolls per obstacle, humans and Full Alert pursuers landing hits, and the round cap at 5.

The real game lies somewhere between generous and strict. Where a number moves a lot between them, the rules are underspecified at that point.

## 1. Top findings

1. **By the book, a crew of five almost can't lose these heists. That's a rules finding, not a simulator bug.** Default-reading win rate by heist: 1 100.0% · 2 100.0% · 3 100.0% · 4 99.9% · 5 100.0%. Even reading *every* ambiguous rule against the crew (strict) leaves 77.4% pooled (generous: 100.0%). The cause is the dice. A Failure needs zero Successes. The mean final pool is 9.0 dice against a mean Difficulty of 2.46, so 0.28% of rolls fail and 51.6% are Criticals. A specialist brings Attribute 4–5 + Skill 3. Free bonus dice then stack on top at 2.7 per roll: Assist (minimum 1 die), Make a Scene +2, Tactical Feed +1 every round, I Called It, intel, Boost, Decoy. I checked the pools against the rules roll by roll (trace in §15), and the three table playtests saw the same 10–14-die pools and Alert peaking around 3/10.
2. **The Alert barely moves, because Criticals and cancels undo most rises.** Per heist (default), Alert rises total 2.81. Criticals take back 1.83, and cancels (Plausible Deniability, Smoke and Mirrors, Abort Abort, That's Not What Happened, Damage Control) stop another 0.48 before they land. Ruling B28's "Difficulty 2+" floor doesn't help: 7–8 dice at D2 are Criticals 50–64% of the time. The Escape reducers stack (Escape Routes + I Know a Way, `ESCAPE_STACK_D1` in 15.0% of heists), pushing Escape rolls to D1, where one Success is a full success (`PARTIAL_IMPOSSIBLE`). A Critical at Alert 0 is wasted in 78.1% of heists.
3. **The ambiguous rules that matter most (§7a).** Each is switched from its generous to its strict reading, with every other rule strict: `obstacleSteps` −16.0 pts (worst: The Pet Store Problem 72% → 35%); `creatureScope` −15.8 pts (worst: The Pet Store Problem 80% → 35%); `groupRolls` −9.3 pts (worst: The Pet Store Problem 66% → 35%); `sceneIs` −7.3 pts (worst: The Pet Store Problem 57% → 35%); `partialCost` −6.9 pts (worst: The Cookie Situation 90% → 75%). With every other rule at the default reading, none of them moves the win rate by more than 0.6 points. The crew's margin absorbs any single call; the ambiguities only matter once the ST is otherwise tough.
4. **Partial +1 Alert, (a) vs (b).** Default reading: win 100.0% (b) vs 99.8% (a); Full Alert 0.4% vs 2.1%. Strict reading: (b) 84.6% vs (a) 77.4%. By heist, strict: 1 89%→74% · 2 100%→98% · 3 38%→36% · 4 98%→85% · 5 98%→94%. Under (a), Partials add 2.01 Alert per strict heist. It hurts most where the crew must cross individually past a creature (Cookie, Library). Whichever way the book rules on Partials, it should rule on group checks at the same time (next finding).
5. **There is no group-check rule, so one specialist's roll beats the whole crew sneaking.** Where an obstacle can be solved by one spider's roll *or* by everyone rolling (charm vs sneak past the cat, deceive vs sneak past the librarian, jam vs cross the sensor), the crew picks the single roll 99.7% of the time. Five individual Stealth rolls are five chances of a Failure, a Partial or a weak spider stuck at 1–2 dice. Letting one leader roll for the crew (`groupRolls`) is worth +9.3 points strict (Pet Store 35% → 66%). The book implies individual rolls (Drafting: "each crewmate… movement rolls") but never says so.
6. **The heists' difficulty labels don't predict how hard they play. Creatures do.** Strict win rate, hardest first: The Pet Store Problem (standard, Limit 8) 35.5% · The Cookie Situation (easy, Limit 10) 74.2% · The Library Job (hard, Limit 6) 85.2% · The Restaurant Rush (hard, Limit 6) 93.9% · The Office After Hours (standard, Limit 8) 98.4%. Per-round creature Alert per strict heist: 1 8.4 · 2 0.3 · 3 11.5 · 4 3.1 · 5 0.0. The "Easy" Cookie heist has a cat awake from obstacle 1, and the "Standard" Pet Store has two creatures the text makes aware on sight. The "Hard" Restaurant has only humans and a rat with no per-round Alert. Humans never add Alert per round and have no attack, so human-only locations are the safest. `CREATURE_ALONE_FULL_ALERT`: 8.4% of strict heists. The Limit ≤ 6 locations also skip the Alert 7 escalations entirely (the cat never hunts at Limit 6) and the Lockdown penalty (`FULL_ALERT_NO_LOCKDOWN`).
7. **Planning and a few abilities delete whole obstacles with no roll.** A Silk Line pre-placed during Planning clears the Cookie cabinet climb in 100.0% of heists (`SILK_LINE_BYPASS`; the text says "Acrobatics to climb"). No-roll gadgets clear the Office's motion sensors in 85.8% and its locked drawer ("Engineering moment") in 68.4% of heists. That's I Made a Thing: Bypass every scene, plus the Spitting Spider's jam, which has no usage limit at all (`MECH_BYPASS` 31.5% of default heists). Casing is a formality: 12.7 Successes per heist for 3 facts (`CASING_OVERFLOW` 99.9%), with no cost for failing. Familiar Face can even reveal the "unknown" obstacle.
8. **Loss is effectively impossible, and the Waiting Web almost never activates.** Loss: default 0.00%, strict 0.00%. Spiders Out per heist: default 0.000, strict 0.007, even with threats attacking every round and humans and Full Alert pursuers landing hits (strict). Ch 10 says "a solid hit from a real threat will get through more often than not", but only 30.5% of hits land. Attack pools are 3–4 dice (Pounce 4, Strike 4, Brawl 3) against BODY + Endurance of 4–8, ties go to the defender, and a level comes back every obstacle. Structural gaps the dice rarely reach: a spider Out in the last obstacle never gets a replacement; Alert past the Limit does nothing (`ALERT_PAST_LIMIT` 49.6% strict); a Full Alert Escape has no pursuit rule; and humans have no stat block to roll with or against.
9. **Silk Points are mostly unspent.** 90.6% of spiders (default) and 82.5% (strict) end with ≥ 75% of their starting Silk. Counting Silk earned mid-heist, crews finish holding 100.6% as much Silk as they started with (default). The rolls rarely need it. Policy matters only under strict: `silkPolicy` hoard −7.6 pts, spendy +12.5 pts. The biggest spends are Improvise (the weak spiders' way through Stealth) and the 1-SP Silk Line. Compulsive Planner is Silk-neutral by construction (`COMPULSIVE_NET_ZERO`).
10. **Dead content, and what the playtest fixes would do.** Never or almost never fires in these five heists: Fear of Vacuums (no vacuum anywhere), Butterfingers (a sensible crew never hands its spider the loot), Loud and Arachnophobe Magnet (the crew avoids Stealth near humans and high Alert), Contingency (it triggers on a Failure, and those almost never happen), Field Repair, and the Jumping Spider and Orb Weaver abilities (no gaps; the climb is pre-lined). See §6. Show-Off's "Difficulty 4 version" would make a Difficulty 5 roll easier. Balance outliers (strict win rate of crews that include it vs comparable crews, §6; ±2.5 pts or more): perk:plausible-deniability +4.2, perk:tactical-feed +2.9, role:grifter +2.8, role:bruiser −3.3, flaw:allergic-to-dust −4.6. Fix experiments (§8; baseline strict 77.9%, default 100.0%): critAlertRule=baseD3: strict 71.2%, default 100.0%; bonusDiceCap=3: strict 76.9%, default 100.0%; spittingLimit=scene: strict 77.9%, default 100.0%; all three fixes: strict 70.2%, default 100.0%. None of them restores real risk under the default reading. The Spitting limit changes nothing here because each heist has at most one sensor. The root causes are the dice-pool-to-Difficulty ratio and the missing group-check and Partial rules.

### 7c. Parameter definitions

- **`partialCost`** — What a Partial Success's complication costs. Default `"setback"`; alternatives `"alert"`, `"none"`; strict `"alert"`, generous `"none"`. alert = +1 Alert (variant a: what the Foundry system suggests, HEISTY.results.partial.alert = 1). setback = no Alert; the spider takes −1 die on its next roll (variant b: the Alert table lists no Partial trigger, but the complication must 'actually cost you something'; −1 die for a round is Ch 10's own 'glancing bump'). none = no mechanical cost. *(Ch 2 Types of Results; Ch 9 What Raises the Alert; Ch 21)*
- **`groupRolls`** — Who rolls when the whole crew must get past something. Default `"individual"`; alternatives `"leader"`; strict `"individual"`, generous `"leader"`. individual = every spider rolls for itself on movement/stealth obstacles (implied by Drafting 'each crewmate gets +1 die on movement rolls'). leader = one spider rolls for the crew. *(Ch 2 (no group-roll rule); Ch 5 Drafting / Escape Routes / I Know a Way)*
- **`creatureRolls`** — How creature obstacles are rolled. Default `"static"`; alternatives `"opposed"`; strict `"opposed"`, generous `"static"`. static = Ch 15 Toolkit Difficulty. opposed = spider vs the creature's Ch 16 pool (most Successes wins, ties to the world; no Partial or Critical exists for an opposed roll). *(Ch 2 Opposed Rolls; Ch 15 vs Ch 16; Ch 19 Heist 2 'First real opposed roll')*
- **`creatureWake`** — When a creature counts as active (and adds its per-round Alert). Default `"escalation"`; alternatives `"hunt"`; strict `"escalation"`, generous `"hunt"`. escalation = from the first Escalation step that wakes it (cat 3, snake 3, parrot 5) or when the heist text says it is awake/aware. hunt = only when hunting/pursuing (cat 7…) or when the text says so. *(Ch 9 +X row / ruling B22; Ch 14 patrol example; Ch 16 Escalation lines)*
- **`creatureScope`** — Where an active creature's per-round Alert applies. Default `"presence"`; alternatives `"obstacle"`, `"location"`; strict `"location"`, generous `"obstacle"`. presence = a mobile creature (the cat; a hunting snake; an aware guard spider) adds its Alert every round anywhere once active; caged/static ones only at their own obstacle. obstacle = only at its own obstacle. location = every active creature every round, everywhere (the most literal reading). *(Ch 9 '+X A creature's own contribution each round it's active')*
- **`creatureStaysActive`** — Does an awake creature stay awake?. Default `true`; alternatives `false`; strict `true`, generous `false`. true = once active, active for the rest of the heist (the Alert only goes up). false = active only while its Escalation threshold (or its own obstacle's text) says so. *(Ch 16 Escalation (no 'goes back to sleep' rule))*
- **`failureAttack`** — Does a Failure near an active threat get you hit?. Default `true`; alternatives `false`; strict `true`, generous `false`. true = on a Failure/Botch, an active threat present at the obstacle attacks the spider (Ch 10 Taking Hits). false = Failure only costs the +1 Alert. *(Ch 2 'something gets worse'; Ch 10 Taking Hits)*
- **`threatTurn`** — When threats attack. Default `"onFailure"`; alternatives `"everyRound"`; strict `"everyRound"`, generous `"onFailure"`. onFailure = a threat lands a hit only as the 'something worse' of a failed roll. everyRound = in the threat phase of every round, each engaged threat (active at its own obstacle, hunting, or pursuing at Full Alert) attacks a spider still exposed (Ch 3 'then the ST runs the threats'). *(Ch 3 Turn Order; Ch 10 Taking Hits; Ch 16 'When a creature acts against the crew')*
- **`partialHit`** — Can a Partial's complication be a hit?. Default `false`; alternatives `true`; strict `true`, generous `false`. true = on a Partial next to an engaged threat, the threat also lands an attack (the ST's 'funniest complication that actually costs you something'). *(Ch 2 Partial Success)*
- **`humanAttackPool`** — Do humans land hits?. Default `0`; alternatives `3`; strict `3`, generous `0`. Humans have no stat block or attack pool (Ch 16 points to Ch 15's Human Obstacles, which have only Difficulties). 0 = they never hit. 3 = a Failure at a human obstacle draws a 3-dice hit (the Human With Broom). *(Ch 15 Human Obstacles; Ch 16 closing line)*
- **`fullAlertPursuit`** — Does a Full Alert Escape have teeth?. Default `0`; alternatives `4`; strict `4`, generous `0`. 0 = only creatures with attack pools can hurt the crew. 4 = at Full Alert every failed Escape roll draws a 4-dice hit from 'the location' (humans with brooms, a second vacuum…). *(Ch 9 'Escape is the only play, and it will be memorable'; Ch 13 'play the lockdown')*
- **`maxRounds`** — How many rounds an obstacle can take before the ST ends it. Default `10`; alternatives `5`, `20`; strict `5`, generous `20`. Failure is 'no progress' and nothing in the rules ends a run of failures except the Alert. After this many rounds: a heist obstacle is abandoned (objective lost if not yet taken); spiders stuck in the Escape are left behind (Out). *(Ch 2 'Failure is a beat, not a wall'; Ch 13 'Make failure a door')*
- **`obstacleSteps`** — How many successful rolls a heist obstacle needs. Default `1`; alternatives `2`, `3`; strict `2`, generous `1`. 1 = one roll that meets it (a Partial counts: 'the lock opens, but you made noise'). 2 or 3 = the ST calls for a roll at each beat (approach, work, get clear) — each needs a Partial or better. Applies to single-roll obstacles; individual obstacles still need one pass per spider. *(Ch 11 'each with a clear goal'; Ch 2 'When Not to Roll' — the book never says how many rolls an obstacle is)*
- **`crewSize`** — Crew size. Default `5`; alternatives `3`. The brief is a crew of 5; the book supports 2–6 players. *(Ch 1 What You Need)*
- **`intelValue`** — What a piece of casing intel is worth. Default `"firstRoll"`; alternatives `"none"`, `"allRolls"`; strict `"none"`, generous `"allRolls"`. firstRoll = +1 die on each spider's first roll at an obstacle the crew has intel on. none = information only. allRolls = +1 die on every roll there. (A known climb also lets the crew pre-place a Silk Line — Ch 11 Preparation.) *(Ch 11 Casing ('information, not a free win'); Ch 17)*
- **`silkLineBypass`** — Does a Silk Line skip climb/gap rolls?. Default `true`; alternatives `false`; strict `false`, generous `true`. true = as written: a Silk Line (1 SP, or GRACE+Acrobatics D2) is safe footing 'with no Acrobatics roll' for the crew. false = the ST still calls for rolls. *(Ch 8 Silk Line; Ch 12 Silk Is the Universal Tool; Ch 11 Preparation)*
- **`slipperyDoubleMove`** — Slippery Surface: double movement instead of a roll. Default `true`; alternatives `false`; strict `false`, generous `true`. true = when no active threat is present, the crew takes double movement instead of rolling (Ch 3 / Ch 15 'costs double movement or a roll'). *(Ch 3 Slick surfaces; Ch 15 Slippery Surface)*
- **`cleanFailAlert`** — Does a clean failure on the Botch die add the normal +1?. Default `0`; alternatives `1`; strict `1`, generous `0`. 0 = 'a clean failure, no bonus disaster' adds nothing (rules.mjs). 1 = it is still a Failure and adds the Failure's +1. *(Ch 2 The Botch)*
- **`improviseAttr`** — Which Attribute an Improvised Skill uses. Default `"skill"`; alternatives `"called"`; strict `"called"`, generous `"skill"`. skill = the swapped-in Skill's own Attribute. called = the Attribute the ST originally called for. *(Ch 8 Improvise)*
- **`sceneIs`** — What a 'scene' is (for once-per-scene abilities). Default `"obstacle"`; alternatives `"phase"`; strict `"phase"`, generous `"obstacle"`. obstacle = every obstacle is a scene. phase = the Heist is one scene and the Escape another. *('Once per scene' appears ~20 times; 'scene' is never defined)*
- **`silkPolicy`** — Crew Silk Point spending policy. Default `"greedy"`; alternatives `"hoard"`, `"spendy"`. greedy = the book's advice: hoard early, prevent failures as the Alert nears the Limit, Clutch/Damage Control near the Limit. hoard = only Clutch/Damage Control to stop a Full Alert. spendy = spend whenever it helps. *(Ch 8 Silk Point Management)*
- **`lootCarrier`** — Who carries the loot out. Default `"safest"`; alternatives `"taker"`. safest = the crew hands the loot to its toughest spider without Butterfingers. taker = whoever grabbed it keeps it. *(Not covered)*
- **`lootOnOut`** — What happens to the loot when its carrier goes Out. Default `"pickup"`; alternatives `"lost"`; strict `"lost"`, generous `"pickup"`. pickup = it drops; another spider spends an Action to grab it (Butterfingers' own rule). lost = it goes with the carrier (jarred, vacuumed…). *(Not covered (Ch 10 Going Out; Ch 7 Butterfingers))*
- **`escapeCount`** — Escape obstacles per heist. Default `2`; alternatives `1`. Ch 11 says 1–2; the ready-to-run heists give none, so the simulator generates them. *(Ch 11 Phase 4; Ch 19)*
- **`escapeDiffShift`** — Escape Difficulty. Default `0`; alternatives `1`; strict `1`, generous `0`. 0 = Easy location 2, otherwise 3. 1 = one harder. *(Not given anywhere)*
- **`spectacularFailure`** — Chance a Botch earns +1 SP (Spectacular Failure, ST's call). Default `0.25`; alternatives `0`, `0.75`; strict `0`, generous `0.75`. The table has to laugh. Applied to Botches only; ordinary Failures earn 1/5 of this. *(Ch 8 Earning Silk Points)*
- **`creativeSpecies`** — Chance a species-ability use earns +1 SP (Creative Species Use). Default `0.2`; alternatives `0`, `0.6`; strict `0`, generous `0.6`. 'in a way nobody saw coming' — ST's call. *(Ch 8 Earning Silk Points)*
- **`brilliantPlan`** — Chance the crew's plan earns +2 SP (Brilliant Plan). Default `0.2`; alternatives `0`, `0.8`; strict `0`, generous `0.8`. Per heist, to the spider who pitches it. *(Ch 8 Earning Silk Points)*
- **`flawTiming`** — When the ST fires once-per-heist Flaws. Default `"random"`; alternatives `"worst"`; strict `"worst"`, generous `"random"`. random = a random obstacle. worst = at the objective obstacle ('the worst available time', Ch 1). *(Ch 7 Flaws ('the ST picks when'))*
- **`exterminator`** — Does the ST call the Exterminator at Lockdown?. Default `false`; alternatives `true`. Only possible where the Limit is above 7 (Heists 1–3). *(Ch 16 The Exterminator)*
- **`midHeistComplications`** — Chance per obstacle of a Ch 20 Mid-Heist Complication. Default `0`; alternatives `0.5`. d6 table: cat wakes; drawer; human up; vacuum (Fear of Vacuums); phone; +1 Alert. *(Ch 20 Mid-Heist Complication)*
- **`bypassComplexLocks`** — Does 'I Made a Thing: Bypass' beat the antique lock?. Default `false`; alternatives `true`; strict `false`, generous `true`. Bypass defeats 'one small mechanical obstacle… pop a simple latch'. false = not the library's antique lock (Difficulty 4). true = any lock. *(Ch 5 The Tinkerer)*
- **`spittingJamsLocks`** — Does the Spitting Spider's strand defeat locks and lids?. Default `false`; alternatives `true`; strict `false`, generous `true`. false = jamming defeats sensors only (a jammed lock stays locked). true = it also pops latches/lids/locks. The ability has no usage limit either way. *(Ch 4 Precision Application)*
- **`assistFromPassed`** — Can a spider that already got past help one still crossing?. Default `true`; alternatives `false`; strict `false`, generous `true`. Assist needs adjacency; true = yes (they wait at the edge), false = no. *(Ch 2 Assists; Ch 3 Adjacency)*
- **`replacementResets`** — Do Waiting Web replacements get fresh once-per-heist uses?. Default `false`; alternatives `true`; strict `false`, generous `true`. false = once per heist is per player. true = a new spider has its own. *(Ch 10 Going Out)*
- **`nptpScope`** — 'Not Part of the Plan' once per heist — per spider or per crew?. Default `"spider"`; alternatives `"crew"`; strict `"crew"`, generous `"spider"`. Damage Control says 'the crew… once per heist'; Not Part of the Plan just says once per heist. *(Ch 8 Spending Silk Points)*
- **`activeBandStealth`** — Does the Stirring Stealth +1 continue through Active (5–6)?. Default `1`; alternatives `0`; strict `1`, generous `0`. 1 = yes (config.mjs; the Storyteller Note says the Stirring and Lockdown penalties stack at 7+). 0 = the Active row read literally: 'No roll penalty yet'. *(Ch 9 Alert Thresholds vs its Storyteller Note; Ch 21)*
- **`critAlertRule`** — FIX EXPERIMENT — when a Critical lowers the Alert. Default `"book"`; alternatives `"baseD3"`. book = final Difficulty 2+ (ruling B28). baseD3 = only on a base Difficulty of 3+ (before reducers), at most once per obstacle, never after Full Alert (the playtests' proposed fix). *(Ch 2 Critical Success; playtest issues H1-16 / H2-I-13)*
- **`bonusDiceCap`** — FIX EXPERIMENT — cap on bonus dice from all non-Silk sources. Default `99`; alternatives `3`. 99 = no cap (the book). 3 = Assist, intel, Make a Scene, Tactical Feed, I Called It, Boost, Decoy… add at most +3 dice to one roll (playtest proposal). *(Ch 2 Assists; Ch 5; playtest issue H1-18)*
- **`spittingLimit`** — FIX EXPERIMENT — Spitting Spider jam frequency. Default `"none"`; alternatives `"scene"`. none = no usage limit (as written). scene = once per scene like every other active species ability. *(Ch 4 Precision Application; playtest issue H2-I-10)*
- **`crewComposition`** — Crew building. Default `"distinct"`; alternatives `"ch20"`. distinct = five different Roles (the book: 'the crew wants variety'). ch20 = every spider rolled on the Ch 20 tables. *(Ch 7; Ch 20)*

## 12. What the book leaves undefined

Each entry gives the book reference and what the simulator did. Parameters are in §7; counts are in §9.

- **No group-check rule** *(Ch 2; Ch 5 Drafting ('each crewmate… movement rolls'))* — When the whole crew must sneak past or climb something, the book never says whether each spider rolls, one leader rolls, or the NPC rolls once. Drafting / Escape Routes / I Know a Way imply everyone rolls. → `groupRolls`
- **Whether a Partial raises the Alert** *(Ch 2 Types of Results; Ch 9 table; Ch 21)* — Partial = 'a complication lands… that actually costs you something', but the Alert table lists no Partial trigger. The Foundry system suggests +1. → `partialCost`
- **How many rolls / attempts an obstacle is** *(Ch 11 Phase 3; Ch 2 When Not to Roll)* — An obstacle has 'a clear goal', but the book never says whether one success clears it, or what ends a run of Failures ('no progress') other than the Alert. → `obstacleSteps / maxRounds` · detector `STUCK_OBSTACLE`
- **Fixed Difficulty vs opposed roll for creatures** *(Ch 15 Toolkit vs Ch 16 pools; Ch 2 Opposed Rolls)* — The same cat is 'Stealth (3)' in Ch 15 and 'Perception 4' in Ch 16. Opposed rolls have no Partial or Critical, and Alert-band / cover Difficulty modifiers have nothing to act on (the simulator ignores them in opposed mode). → `creatureRolls` · detector `OPPOSED_NO_PARTIAL`
- **When a creature is 'active' and where its +X applies** *(Ch 9 +X row; ruling B22; Ch 14; Ch 16)* — 'Awake, hunting or pursuing' — the cat's Escalation says 'ears rotate' at 3, Ch 14 says the Alert reaching 3 wakes it, and Heist 1 says it is awake from the start. Whether a mobile creature's per-round Alert counts in other rooms is not said, and nothing puts a creature back to sleep. → `creatureWake / creatureScope / creatureStaysActive` · detector `CREATURE_DROVE_FULL_ALERT`
- **Does the Stirring Stealth +1 continue at Active (5–6)?** *(Ch 9 table ('No roll penalty yet') vs its Storyteller Note ('the Stirring penalty and the Lockdown penalty both apply'))* — config.mjs keeps +1; the table row says no penalty. → `activeBandStealth`
- **What 'a scene' is** *(~20 'once per scene' abilities)* — Never defined. Per-obstacle scenes make Make a Scene, Phase Through, I Made a Thing, Fast Talk, Plausible Deniability… available at every obstacle. → `sceneIs`
- **Taking a hit when BODY + Endurance − penalty ≤ 0** *(Ch 10 Taking Hits; Ch 2 The Botch)* — The Botch die gives no Successes to compare; treated as 0. · detector `DEFENSE_POOL_ZERO`
- **Do Vitality penalties reduce Assist dice?** *(Ch 2 Assists ('Skill only… min 1'); Ch 10 ('−N dice on all rolls'))* — Treated as yes, then the minimum of 1 applies. · detector `ASSIST_PENALTY`
- **A Critical spider nobody can carry** *(Ch 3 Speed; Ch 10 Critical (ruling B2))* — 'Must be assisted to move' — nothing says what happens if nobody is free (left behind? caught?). The simulator counts it as left behind (Out) when the obstacle hits the round cap. · detector `CRITICAL_NO_CARRIER`

## 13. What is modelled

**Modelled:** dice pools (Attribute + Skill + bonuses − Vitality), Successes on 4–6, Difficulty 1–5, Full/Partial/Failure/Critical, the Botch die; Alert bands (Stirring/Active Stealth +1, Lockdown all +1 and Stealth +2), Full Alert at the Limit (objective lost if not taken; skip to the Escape after finishing the round); Critical −1 Alert at Difficulty 2+; Failure +1; Botch +2; spider Out +2; creature per-round contributions; guard 'spotted' +1; parrot shriek +2; rat deal +2; one Assist per roll (Skill only, 1–3 dice); opposed rolls (variant); Silk spends: extra die, Silk Line, Reroll, Improvise, Silk Clutch, Damage Control, Not Part of the Plan, That's Not What Happened; earns: Flaw Moments, Spectacular Failure, Creative Species Use, Brilliant Plan, Show-Off Critical; Vitality, Taking Hits (BODY+Endurance vs the attack; ties to the defender; −1/−2 levels), Recovery (quiet round or gap, once per obstacle), carrying Critical spiders; Going Out, Waiting Web replacements (Rattled, half Silk, next obstacle), Loss = everyone Out at once; Casing (one Perception/Tactics roll each, one fact per Success), Preparation (pre-placed Silk Lines, Contingency); Debrief AP (full / half rounded down / 0); species: Jumping (auto-leap on gaps), Orb Weaver (Line), Wolf (Run It Again), Cellar (squeeze), Spitting (jam), Crab (see not modelled); Signature Moves: all seven; Perks: Silver Tongue, Read the Room, Plausible Deniability, Fast Talk, Actually I Planned This, Familiar Face, Silk Trail, Soundless, Second-Story Spider, Jury-Rig, Spider-Sense, Overclock, Field Repair, I See How This Works, Take the Hit, Unfazed, Thunderous Entrance, Negotiating Position, That All You Got?, Early Warning, Tactical Feed, Contingency, Escape Routes, Counter-Surveillance, Don't Look Down, Passenger (carrying only), Drafting, Abort Abort, Method Actor, The Long Con, Smoke and Mirrors; Flaws: all ten (Fear of Vacuums only via the optional Ch 20 complication table).

**Not modelled (and why):**

- *Crab Spider — Wait, Was That There Before?* — needs a full turn holding still; every obstacle here needs the spider to move, so it never applies (movement is abstracted away)
- *Orb Weaver — Snare* — needs a creature to walk into a webbed square; there is no grid
- *Ghost Protocol* — the book resolves spotting as the spider's own Stealth roll, so 'when you're spotted' has no separate moment to trigger in
- *I Was Never Here* — affects NPCs remembering you after the heist; no mechanical effect during a job
- *Dead Drop, Planted Evidence, Shortcut, Trap Architect* — spatial Planning tricks with no defined mechanical payoff (Trap Architect: what a trapped NPC suffers is undefined)
- *Pattern Recognition* — knowing a patrol's next three actions only matters on a grid; treated as redundant with casing intel
- *Silk Grapple* — 'held one round' has no defined effect on an obstacle
- *Double Bluff, Quick Change* — disguise-specific timing that the obstacle abstraction doesn't create
- *Always a Way Out* — 'one option left' has no mechanical definition
- *Web Structure (2 SP)* — no obstacle in the ready-to-run heists is framed around one; Silk Line covers the climb/gap case
- *Flaw delay (1 SP to push a Flaw back a round)* — only shifts timing; not worth a decision model
- *Speed, distances, line of sight, cover* — no grid: obstacles are abstract. Cover's +2 to spot you would otherwise be a further bypass

## 14. Character generator

750000 spiders built for the headline runs (specialist 50%, generalist 25%, random 25%). Each one is checked with `finalAttributes`, `attributesSpent` and `skillBudget` from module/logic/rules.mjs. Rules the generator could not satisfy: **none**. Pack/config consistency: consistent.

Policies: *specialist* (role Attributes weighted ×6, both core skills 3, three more skills at 3), *generalist* (no Attribute above 4, core skills 2, the rest spread at 1–2), *random* (uniform). Crew: five different Roles, random species (param `crewComposition`).
