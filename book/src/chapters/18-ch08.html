Chapter 8

Silk Points

Silk Points are the economy of the heist. They represent your spider’s preparation, focus, and the specific desperate improvisation that only happens when you’re three squares from the prize and the vacuum just switched on.

You start with WIT + NERVE Silk Points. When they’re gone, they’re gone — there’s no mid-heist refill. There’s only spending well, earning a few back through spectacular failure, and rationing the rest.

Spending Silk Points

CostEffect
1 SP — Extra DieAdd 1 die to a roll before it’s made. The most common spend, and a good one.
1 SP — Silk LineInstantly run a silk line between two points up to 5 squares apart, for climbing, swinging, or rigging. No roll.
2 SP — RerollAfter rolling, reroll up to 3 dice and keep the better result. For when the dice have wronged you personally.
2 SP — Web StructureBuild a small web structure with no roll — a net, a tripwire, a platform, a hammock. The hammock has no tactical use. The Tinkerer built it anyway.
2 SP — ImproviseSwap the called-for Skill for one of yours that could plausibly work — Engineering to rig a hoist instead of Athletics to climb, Deception instead of Stealth — at +1 Difficulty. A last resort for working outside your specialty.
3 SP — Silk ClutchAfter a failed roll, spend 3 SP to succeed anyway. The Alert rises by 1. You did it, at a cost.
3 SP — Damage ControlThe crew spends 3 SP together to reduce one Alert spike of +2 or more by 1. Once per heist. Call it the moment the spike triggers, before it’s added.
4 SP — Not Part of the PlanNegate one complication the ST just introduced — used immediately, before consequences resolve. Once per heist. The complication doesn’t happen. You explain later how you accounted for it. You didn’t.

Silk Point Management

The classic mistake is spending Silk Points early on small problems and having none when the Alert is at 7 and the cat has found the bag. Start conservative; spend big when it matters. Your strategy should shift as the Alert climbs: early on, hoard — small failures aren’t worth big spends. As the Alert nears its limit, switch to preventing failures rather than recovering from them. A 1 SP die before a roll at Alert 6 is worth more than a 3 SP Silk Clutch after a roll at Alert 8.
