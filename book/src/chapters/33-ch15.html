Chapter 15

The Obstacle Toolkit

An obstacle is a specific challenge between the crew and the objective — a clear goal, a success outcome, a failure outcome. Here’s a working toolkit. Difficulties assume a calm location; the Alert raises them as it climbs.

Creature Obstacles

ObstaclePrimary RollNotes
House CatStealth or Persuasion (3)A sleeping cat stays asleep. An awake one must be distracted, charmed (hard), routed around, or survived.
The DogAthletics or Deception (4)Excellent nose. Distraction works well. Do not engage directly — the Bruiser especially.
The GoldfishPersuasion (1)Will cooperate. Has a three-second memory. Cannot actually help with anything.
Guard SpiderBrawl or Stealth (3)Another spider, hired. Knows what you are and what you’re doing.
Curious ChildDeception or Stealth (3)Children look directly at things. Dangerous. Easily distracted by anything shiny (Difficulty 2).
The ExterminatorAny (4–5)Came prepared. If the Alert got this high, something already went very wrong.

Environmental Obstacles

ObstaclePrimary RollNotes
VacuumAthletics or Tactics (2)Predictable but relentless. Know the route and it’s avoidable. Fear of Vacuums applies.
Motion SensorEngineering or Stealth (3)A beam across a line of squares. Don’t cross it — or jam it (Engineering), or cross it very, very slowly.
Sticky TrapAcrobatics or Perception (2)A glue trap on the floor. Perception to spot it in time; Acrobatics if you didn’t.
The GapAthletics or Acrobatics (2–4)A distance too wide to step across. Difficulty scales with the number of squares and what’s below.
Slippery SurfaceAcrobatics (2)Steel counters, glass, anything the human cleaned recently. Costs double movement or a roll.
Locked DrawerEngineering (3)It’s locked. The Tinkerer’s problem. Or the Bruiser’s, which raises the Alert.

Human Obstacles

ObstaclePrimary RollNotes
Sleeping HumanStealth (2)Asleep and fine — stay quiet. At Alert 5 they stir; at 7 they’re up and turning on lights.
Distracted HumanStealth (1)On their phone, watching TV. Use the window. It closes.
Alert HumanDeception or Stealth (4)Looking for something. Possibly for you. Do not get spotted.
Human With BroomAthletics (3)The broom has already swung once. Keep moving.
