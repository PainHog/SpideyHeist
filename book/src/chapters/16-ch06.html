Chapter 6

Attributes & Skills

Your spider has four Attributes and twelve Skills. When you roll, you add the relevant Attribute to the relevant Skill. That is the whole of the math.

The Four Attributes

AttributeWhat It Covers
BODYPhysical power and toughness. Brawling, sprinting, absorbing hits, carrying things that are technically too heavy.
WITIntelligence and observation. Engineering, spotting what others miss, tactics, planning.
NERVEComposure and social confidence, including lying to a cat. Stealth, deception, intimidation.
GRACEAgility and dexterity. Acrobatics, disguise, persuasion. (Persuasion is GRACE because charm is physical.)

The Twelve Skills

Skills run 0–5. A 0 means you’ve never really tried. A 5 means that, for a spider, you’re world-class.

SkillAttributeCoversExamples
AthleticsBODYRunning, climbing, jumping, hauling.Sprinting a counter, carrying the loot bag
BrawlBODYFighting, grappling, applying spider to problem.Handling the cat up close, breaking something small
EnduranceBODYTaking hits, staying up, working hurt.Absorbing a hit, pushing on while Hurt
EngineeringWITBuilding, breaking, fixing, understanding mechanisms.Picking locks, building gadgets, jamming sensors
PerceptionWITNoticing things, reading a room, spotting danger.Finding a patrol, spotting an ambush, casing a location
TacticsWITPlanning, coordinating, thinking ahead.Directing the crew, finding the route, escape planning
StealthNERVEMoving silently, staying unseen.Sneaking past the cat, crossing a lit room

IntimidationNERVEMaking things afraid of you. Yes, as a spider.Spooking a smaller creature, making a human hesitate
AcrobaticsGRACEPrecision movement, tumbling, tight spaces.Swinging on silk, crossing a gap, threading a squeeze
PersuasionGRACEWinning people over with charm.Talking a cat into a nap, negotiating with another spider
DisguiseGRACEAltering appearance, performing false identities.Playing a dust bunny, hiding in plain sight

Skill Ratings

RatingMeaning
0 — UntrainedNever really done it. Your pool is just the Attribute. Hope it’s high.
1 — DabblerTried it a few times. Rough idea.
2 — CompetentReliable under normal circumstances. The cat being normal circumstances.
3 — SkilledThis is your thing. People call on you for it.
4 — ExpertOne of the best in the building. Maybe the block.
5 — LegendaryMaximum spider. When you fail at this, it’s the dice’s fault.
