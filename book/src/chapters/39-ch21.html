The Grid

RuleDetail
Scale1 square = 1 spider. Distances are counted in squares.
SpeedSquares per turn, set by species. Most 5 · Wolf 7 · Jumping 6 · Crab 4.
Your turnMove up to Speed (may split) + one Action (sneaking is one; reactions aren’t). Diagonals count as 1.
AdjacencyThe 8 surrounding squares. Brawl and Assist need adjacency.
CoverA solid object between you and a viewer: your Stealth Difficulty −2; +2 Difficulty to target you. Glass blocks silk, not sight.
ClimbingNormal cost on walls/ceilings. Slick/hostile surfaces cost double or a roll. Squeezes and Silk Lines need a roll only under pressure.
HeightsIn squares: chair 2, counter 3, shelf 6, ceiling 8. A real fall: a hit of 1 die per 2 squares.
Hurt/CriticalHurt: Speed halved (round down) · Critical: moves only with a crewmate’s help, at half their Speed.
Turn orderOnly when timing is tight: crew acts (any order), then the ST’s threats.

The Five Phases

PhaseSummary
1 — The ScoreST presents the job and the visible map. Roleplay only. No rolls, no Alert.
2 — PlanningCase for intel (a Success = one true detail, never the unknown). Make Preparations (one per spider; setup, not auto-wins, except a Contingency; each gets a complication).
3 — The Heist3–4 obstacles, two successful rolls each. The Alert climbs. At least one thing the crew didn’t uncover.
4 — The Escape1–2 obstacles. Alert carries over. At least one must be solvable without Athletics.
5 — DebriefCount loot, award AP, argue about what happened, take the next job.
