Chapter 16

Creature Compendium

Every major threat has a stat block: what it rolls, how fast it moves in squares, how fast it escalates, and how to run it. When a creature acts against the crew, roll the listed pool and count 5+; that’s what the crew is up against. An Escalation step names an Alert level, and Full Alert counts as every step at once — at a low Limit, a creature can go straight from asleep to hunting.

Driving one off. Spiders don’t win fights with cats; they end them. Clear a creature’s obstacle with Brawl or Intimidation and it backs off for the rest of that obstacle: no attacks, no +X there. If it roams, it’s back at the next one, sulking. Fights are loud: every roll of one that lands is +1 Alert, and a lost one is a Loud Failure.

House Cat “The Reason We Can’t Have Nice Heists”

Threat Level
Standard
Alert Contribution
+1 per round while active
Speed
8

Perception 4 · Pursuit (Athletics) 5 · Pounce (Brawl) 4

Passive
asleep ~70% of the time. When awake, paying complete attention.
Senses
spots suspicious movement within about 6 squares in line of sight.
Escalation
Alert 3, ears rotate. Alert 5, moves toward the sound. Alert 7, hunting.
Weakness
shiny objects (Difficulty 1 to redirect), sudden loud noises in the opposite direction, and cucumbers. The ST may rule a cucumber a Difficulty 0 distraction if the crew somehow produced one.
Fear
nothing. The cat fears nothing.

House Dog “It Wants to Be Friends. That’s Worse.”

Threat Level
High
Alert Contribution
+2 per round while active (it is loud)
Speed
8

Nose (Perception) 5 · Chase (Athletics) 4 · Bark (Intimidation) 5

Passive
not malicious. Wants to play. Equally threatening.
Senses
smells a spider automatically at Alert 2+; doesn’t need line of sight.
Escalation
at Alert 4 it barks. When it barks, the Alert immediately rises by 2.
Weakness

Vacuum “It Has No Feelings, and That Makes It Worse”

Threat Level
Low–Medium
Alert Contribution
+1 per round in pursuit
Speed
4 (slow, tireless)

Navigation 2 · Pursuit 3 (its attack: whatever it catches takes the hit)

Passive
follows a programmed route. Predictable, loud, covers the same ground on a loop.
Senses
notices nothing. Navigates by bumping into things.
Weakness
furniture legs, small obstacles, and stairs (it stops at stairs and makes a sad noise).
Fear Interaction
any spider with Fear of Vacuums treats it as an active threat no matter what it’s doing. The vacuum is unaware of this. The vacuum is unaware of everything.
Special
a spider that climbs onto the vacuum rides along with it. This is both tactically useful and deeply undignified.

Curious Child “The Most Dangerous Predator in the Location”

Threat Level
High
Alert Contribution
+3 if it catches a spider (pure excitement)
Speed
6

Spotting 4 · Capture Attempt 3 · Shouting for a parent: automatic at Alert 7+

Passive
present during the day, actively hunting for interesting things to catch.
Senses
checks everywhere — under the couch, behind the shelf, directly into corners.
Weakness
television (Difficulty 1), toys (Difficulty 1), snacks (Difficulty 0).
Capture Result

Guard Spider “Professional. Unfortunately.”

Threat Level
High
Alert Contribution
+1 per round aware
Speed
6

Perception 4 · Brawl 3 · Stealth 2 · Deception 1

Passive
usually hired by someone who knew a heist was possible. Patrols actively.
Senses
spots movement within 6 squares in line of sight.
Escalation
aware the moment it spots the crew: +1 Alert (its “spotted,” not an extra), then +1 a round, and it follows the crew. Aware lasts the heist, unless it’s paid off.
Note
cannot be charmed by the Face — professional courtesy. Fast Talk and Deception still work.
Weakness
bribery (the crew must produce something valuable on the spot), the Grifter’s Decoy (works once), the Bruiser (works, but loudly — and a beaten guard calls for backup: a fresh one, already aware, takes its post).
Note
guard spiders aren’t evil. They’re working. Anyone but the Face could try talking to one. The Difficulty is 4. Worth one attempt.

Human “Ten Thousand Times Your Size”

Threat Level
Low (asleep) to High (broom)
Alert Contribution
none of its own
Speed
10

Swat 3 · Perception 3 (its own checks only — a Decoy, a surprise)

Passive
sleeping, distracted, alert or armed with a broom — its Chapter 15 row. You roll against that row’s Difficulty; it never rolls to spot you.
Senses
sees anything in its room not in cover. A sleeper only hears.
Escalation
stirs at 5; up with the lights on at 7 or Full Alert. Its row stays the same.
Note
a glimpse of a spider is “spotted” (+1); loot moving on its own is a confirmed alert (+2). Fail a roll within its reach and you’re swatted (a hit, Swat 3). A Critical spider it hits ends up under a glass: Out.
Weakness
its phone, the TV, and a deep conviction that it imagined you.
